The Mark

Primary · Artboard3 / Upright flat-bottom block M

Senior thinking, system before service. The primary mark is an upright, flat-bottom block M · three vertical stems, chamfered 45° top shoulders, geometric, extra-bold. Chosen for legibility at 16 px favicon and authority at 11 rem hero. Hover the constructor to explode it. Toggle the grid to audit clearspace.

06

Verdict + Next steps

Why Artboard3 wins

  1. Legibility. Flat bottoms + equal stems hold at 16 px favicon and at billboard scale.
  2. Premium trust. Architectural geometry reads senior, systematic · strategy before decoration.
  3. Square rule. Zero rounds. Every radius removed; the system stays sharp across print, steel and pixels.
  4. Scalable. One mark, three lockups (horizontal / stacked / MRJB™), infinite mockups.
  5. Timeless. No trend chamfers, no gradient fills · black, white, paper + a 10% lime strike.
